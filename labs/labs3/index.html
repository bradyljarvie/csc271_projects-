Pesto Gnocchi

My favorite food is Pesto Gnocchi. I have special memories of making it with my mother when I was a kid and since we are italian, this is a household staple of my family. Pesto is a combination of fresh basil, garlic cloves, pine nuts, parmesan cheese, and extra-virgin olive oil. It is my favorite sauce/paste to put on anything to make it taste better. Gnocchi is my favorite type of pasta as well which makes it a good pairing in my eyes. Chicken can also be included in the dish which can enhance the flavors and add a more filling protein option

Ingredients:
Potato Gnocchi - 1 lb
Basil Pesto - 1/2 cup
Parmesan Cheese - Grated or shaved, for serving
Reserved Pasta Water - 2 to 3 tablespoons
Salt and Black Pepper
